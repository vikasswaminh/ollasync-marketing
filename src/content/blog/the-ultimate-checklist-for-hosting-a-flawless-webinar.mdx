### The Infrastructure Dilemma: WebRTC vs. RTMP/HLS

Your webinar platform relies on one of two primary delivery protocols:

1. **Standard RTMP/HLS (HTTP Live Streaming):** Rock-solid reliability and massive scale, but carries a 10 to 30-second broadcast delay. This delay kills real-time interactions, live Q&A execution, and instant polling.
2. **Sub-second WebRTC:** Real-time bi-directional streaming with latency under 500 milliseconds. Essential for interactive product demos, fireside executive panels, and active sales conversations.

The structural challenge has always been cost. WebRTC ingest and distribution require persistent server connections, making legacy platforms like Zoom, ON24, and GoToWebinar gatekeep sub-second latency behind predatory enterprise tiers.

Furthermore, traditional platforms treat global audiences as an afterthought. If a tier-one product launch targets the US, DACH, APAC, and LATAM simultaneously, teams historically had to deploy parallel localized sessions or route expensive third-party audio streams into auxiliary Zoom breakout channels.
