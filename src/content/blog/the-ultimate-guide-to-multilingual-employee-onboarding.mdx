### Why Latency and Contextual Windows Break Traditional AI Pipelines

Building an automated pipeline for **multilingual employee onboarding** isn't just about plugging a basic STT API into an open-source translation model. Standard APIs fail in corporate onboarding due to three distinct failure modes:

1. **Jargon and Domain Lexicons:** Generic models stumble on company-specific acronyms, software stacks, and internal equity structures (e.g., confusing "RSUs" with general financial terms).
2. **Buffer Latency vs. Context Accuracy:** To translate accurately, a neural model needs sentence context. Translating word-by-word yields gibberish; waiting for the end of a long sentence creates an unacceptable 4-second delay. The sweet spot requires continuous streaming text prediction with dynamic clause correction.
3. **Synchronization (Lip-Sync and Audio Ducking):** When delivering translated synthetic voice feeds, the original presenter audio must be ducked intelligently without disorienting the employee.
