# Is there a cheaper alternative to Zooms translated captions add-on?

# Chapter 1: The Direct Answer & Executive Summary

## The Short Answer: Is There a Cheaper Alternative to Zoom’s Translated Captions?

**Yes.** Multiple cheaper—and in many cases, free or significantly more cost-effective—alternatives exist to Zoom’s native Translated Captions add-on. 

Depending on your organization's meeting volume, technical infrastructure, and compliance requirements, the most viable options to replace or bypass Zoom’s $5.00/user/month proprietary add-on include:

1. **Third-Party Chrome Extensions and Overlays (e.g., Tactiq, Felo, Notta):** These tools capture incoming audio via your browser or virtual audio routing, translate it in real time using large language models (LLMs) or neural machine translation (NMT), and project captions directly over your Zoom window. Many offer robust free tiers (e.g., Tactiq’s free tier with 10 monthly meetings) or lower organization-wide costs via shared, non-seat-locked subscriptions.
2. **Usage-Based Enterprise AI Translation Platforms (e.g., Wordly.ai, Interprefy, SyncWords):** Instead of paying fixed monthly seat licenses for every employee who *might* need translation, these platforms charge on a per-minute or per-session basis. For teams with occasional international town halls, webinars, or cross-border sales calls, usage-based translation dramatically lowers the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).
3. **Platform-Level Consolidation (e.g., Microsoft Teams Premium, Google Meet Enterprise):** If your organization already pays for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, switching default video platforms eliminates the standalone add-on fee entirely. Teams Premium integrates live translation across 40+ spoken languages for a flat $7/user/month (which also includes advanced security, custom branding, and intelligent AI recaps), while Google Meet includes live translated captions in select Workspace enterprise tiers at no incremental add-on cost.
4. **Open-Source and Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Pipelines:** Technical teams can run local instances of OpenAI’s Whisper or tap directly into the DeepL API / Google Cloud Translation API via virtual audio cables and open-source caption overlays, effectively reducing translation costs to raw compute pricing (fractions of a cent per minute).
