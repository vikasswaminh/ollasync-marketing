One live training session can serve every market. **Ollasync translates the session into 19+ languages**, so your team can teach once without producing separate recordings.

## Replace localization with live access

Localization often involves translation, voiceover recording, caption editing, and version maintenance. It takes time and budget. With Ollasync, the trainer speaks naturally while each participant chooses translated audio or live captions in a preferred language.

The source material stays consistent, and learners can join from a browser, ask questions, and participate during the same session.

## A repeatable workflow for global teams

1. Prepare one source presentation and invite learners.
2. Start the live session and let participants select a language.
3. Use translated audio or captions during the discussion.
4. Share the AI meeting notes afterward.

This gives the team one teaching workflow and one shared source of truth, without the cost of producing duplicate content.

## Plan for inclusive learning

Speak clearly, pause between ideas, and keep important terms consistent. Live translation helps people follow and contribute. For regulated legal or medical interpretation, use a certified interpreter. For onboarding, enablement, webinars, and collaboration, it provides language access during the session.

## Start with one global session

Run a pilot with a multilingual cohort and measure attendance, participation, and follow-up time. A single session can reach every region in 19+ languages, making global training easier to repeat.
