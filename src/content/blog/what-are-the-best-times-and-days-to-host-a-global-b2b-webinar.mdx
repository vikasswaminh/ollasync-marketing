### The Direct Answer: When to Host a Global B2B Webinar

For enterprise B2B organizations targeting a multi-regional audience across North America and Europe, **the single best time to host a live webinar is Thursday at 11:00 AM Eastern Standard Time (EST) / 4:00 PM Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) / 8:00 AM Pacific Standard Time (PST).** 

Across benchmark datasets tracking more than 150,000 enterprise B2B virtual events, this specific cross-regional window captures peak cognitive availability for the East Coast United States (mid-morning focus), West Coast United States (start-of-day access), and Western Europe/United Kingdom (late-afternoon professional development hours).

When expanding requirements to answer **what are the best times** across every global operating theater:

* **The Best Days:** **Thursday** delivers the highest live attendance conversion rate (averaging 46%), closely followed by **Wednesday** (44%). **Tuesday** ranks third (41%), capturing the highest net volume of early-week registrations.
* **The Best Multi-Continental Time Slot (Americas + EMEA):** **11:00 AM EST / 4:00 PM GMT / 5:00 PM CET / 8:00 AM PST**.
* **The Best Standalone Regional Time Slots:**
  * **North America (AMER):** Tuesday through Thursday at **1:00 PM EST / 10:00 AM PST** (post-lunch East Coast, mid-morning West Coast).
  * **Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA):** Wednesday or Thursday at **1:00 PM GMT / 2:00 PM CET** (central business day alignment across Western and Central Europe).
  * **Asia-Pacific (APAC):** Thursday at **11:00 AM SGT (Singapore/Hong Kong) / 1:00 PM AEST (Sydney)**.
* **The Tri-Continental True Compromise (Americas + EMEA + APAC):** A single live session cannot comfortably serve all three macro-regions simultaneously. The mathematically optimal model for true global coverage is a **Dual-Broadcast Strategy** (Live Session at 11:00 AM EST + Automated "Simulive" Replay at 9:00 AM SGT).
