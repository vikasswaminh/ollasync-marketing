### Why do external attendees face frequent login issues with Teams webinars?
Microsoft Teams relies fundamentally on Azure Active Directory (AAD) tenant architecture. When external guests join an event from a browser or personal device, Teams attempts to authenticate them against their home tenant or force them into an external guest flow. This results in missing chat panels, loop-prompt logins, audio device permission lockouts, and elevated drop-off rates for non-Teams users.

### How does Teams Premium impact webinar capabilities, and is it worth the cost?
Teams Premium adds features like custom branding, advanced registration waitlists, automated reminder emails, and real-time caption translation. However, it does not fix basic architectural issues: external tenant switching, high-concurrency stream latency, or the lack of real-time AI audio dubbing. For organizations running global customer-facing events, paying recurring per-user fees for Teams Premium is worth comparing with a dedicated meeting platform with live translation, such as Ollasync (up to 100 people per meeting).

### Can we run hybrid internal/external webinars on Teams without performance issues?
You can, but it requires careful traffic shaping. Internal users can overwhelm branch office egress points unless you have Microsoft eCDN correctly configured. Meanwhile, external users remain subject to standard ISP routing and regional CDN restrictions, leading to inconsistent presentation quality across different continents.
