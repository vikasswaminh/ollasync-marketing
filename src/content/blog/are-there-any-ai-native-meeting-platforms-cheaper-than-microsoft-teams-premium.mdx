### Architectural Efficiency: Why AI-Native Engines Cost Less to Run

The reason standalone solutions are cheaper than Microsoft Teams Premium comes down to three technical shifts in how audio is captured, processed, and summarized:

#### 1. WebRTC Stream Ingestion vs. Heavy Media Bots
Legacy meeting bots join calls as virtual video participants, consuming multi-stream audio/video pipelines that run up cloud compute bills. Modern AI-native platforms use lightweight client-side hooks, virtual audio drivers, or direct WebRTC real-time transport (RTP) packet extraction. This cuts the cloud infrastructure cost of ingesting a 60-minute call by up to 70%.

#### 2. Tiered Inference & Small Language Models (SLMs)
Microsoft Teams relies heavily on unified calls to large foundational models (such as GPT-4o variants) hosted in Azure. AI-native tools like Fathom, Fireflies.ai, and Read AI deploy a tiered inference pipeline:
* **Audio-to-Text:** Distilled, local, or specialized models (e.g., custom fine-tuned Whisper architectures) run at pennies per compute hour.
* **Extraction & Structuring:** Low-latency 3B–8B parameter SLMs (e.g., Llama 3.2/3.3, Mistral NeMo) handle entity recognition, timestamps, and sentiment scoring.
* **Synthesis:** Frontier models are queried only for final executive summaries, minimizing expensive token generation.

#### 3. Local-First and Hybrid Edge Processing
With the widespread adoption of Neural Processing Units (NPUs) in modern corporate laptops, leading platforms (such as Krisp and Supernormal) push transcription and ambient noise reduction directly to the edge. Edge transcription reduces vendor cloud costs to zero for raw audio processing, allowing providers to pass those savings on through lower subscription tiers.
