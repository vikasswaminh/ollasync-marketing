1. **Reclaim and Reallocate Inactive Licenses:** Identify all provisioned host accounts with zero meetings hosted in the trailing 60 to 90 days. 5. **Enforce Structural Contractual Protections:** Eliminate annual price indexation clauses (standard 3%–7% uplifts), negotiate "True-Forward" billing (prohibiting retrospective mid-term true-ups for temporary spikes), and demand license-swap flexibility (allowing conversion of meeting licenses to phone or contact center seats without contract extensions).
