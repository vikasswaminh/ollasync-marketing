At the edge, the platform renders tailored content variations per attendee segment. Enterprise decision-makers receive customized enterprise case studies and relevant ROI calculations, while mid-market buyers see automated self-service product tours—delivered concurrently across the same live session.
*   **Sub-200ms Latency for Native Bi-Directionality:** Replacing legacy RTMP/HLS pipelines with WebTransport and Ultra-Low Latency WebRTC enables sub-200ms global glass-to-glass latency. This real-time fidelity powers high-stakes audience interaction, instantaneous live-polling aggregation, and dynamic breakout transitions without stream stalls or client-side buffering.
