### The Infrastructure Tax: WebRTC vs. Ultra-Low Latency HLS

Every platform in this tier handles video delivery in one of two ways:

1. **Mesh or Selective Forwarding Unit (SFU) WebRTC:** Sub-second latency, bidirectional media. Highly resource-intensive on cloud compute. At 10,000+ viewers, real-time WebRTC costs skyrocket because the platform processes hundreds of thousands of individual, stateful, bi-directional audio/video streams.
2. **Chunked CMAF / Ultra-Low Latency HLS (LL-HLS):** Latency sits between 2 to 4 seconds. Video is ingested via RTMP/SRT, converted to stream segments, and distributed through global Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) like AWS CloudFront, Cloudflare, or Fastly.

Legacy enterprise platforms built on aging WebRTC infrastructures pass their massive compute bills directly to you. Platforms engineered around edge-distributed LL-HLS push delivery to edge caches, dropping streaming egress costs by up to 80% without introducing noticeable latency for standard keynotes, panels, and Q&A sessions.

Registration limits refer to the total number of individuals who can sign up for your event in your database. Concurrent user limits (CCU) define how many people can actively watch the live stream simultaneously. 

Many budget platforms advertise "unlimited registrations" but aggressively throttle or drop video delivery once your active stream crosses 1,000 or 2,500 concurrent connections. When vetting the cheapest virtual event platforms, always verify the contractual **concurrent viewer limit**, as standard drop-off rates mean a 10,000-attendee registration list typically yields 4,000 to 6,000 peak concurrent viewers.

### Can cheap virtual event platforms integrate with enterprise CRMs like HubSpot or Salesforce?
Yes, provided they support open REST APIs and standard Webhook protocols. Avoid low-cost platforms that lock data behind proprietary silos or demand manual CSV exports. 

A high-performance budget setup passes user registration, attendance duration, engagement tags, and language channel preference to platforms like HubSpot, Marketo, or Salesforce instantly via webhooks. Ollasync and modern alternatives support direct webhook triggers on event registration, stream join, and stream leave, ensuring enterprise data capture remains intact without custom middleware costs.
