### How do we handle lead-sharing without violating GDPR or CCPA?
To stay compliant when determining **how to cohost a** webinar across different jurisdictions:
* Place explicit opt-in checkboxes on the registration landing page: *"I agree to receive communications from both [Company A] and [Guest Expert/Company B]."*
* Link to both organizations' privacy policies directly under the submission form.
* If the influencer does not have a formal corporate structure or DPA (Data Processing Agreement) in place, provide them only with aggregated performance metrics (total attendees, overall company titles) rather than raw PII (emails, phone numbers).

### What is the standard compensation model for B2B influencers?
Influencer compensation generally falls into three models:
1. **Flat Production Fee:** Paid upfront ($1,500 to $10,000 depending on reach). This guarantees their promotional posts, live appearance, and marketing usage rights for 90 days.

3. **Co-Branded Value Exchange:** Common when partnering with SaaS peers rather than independent creators. Neither party pays cash; both parties commit to generating an equal volume of registrants via their existing subscriber lists.
